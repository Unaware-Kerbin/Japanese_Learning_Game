Michi Dojo ships a kanji study list derived from OpenJLPT.

OpenJLPT is a derived work under CC BY-SA 4.0. ShareAlike applies to the
redistributed dataset in src/data/third_party/mountain.json.

Credit:

- Jonathan Waller, JLPT vocabulary and kanji lists (https://www.tanos.co.uk/), CC BY
- Tatoeba example sentences (https://tatoeba.org/), CC BY 2.0 FR

The JLPT band names on these cards are community estimates. They are not the
official lists published after 2010, and finishing a band in this app is not
a JLPT exam result.

Lesson prose in this app is original. The linked video is a pointer, not a copy
of the narration. Reading-shelf links open other sites. This app does not store
their articles.
